The central B.Ed. admissions page requires Bagrut eligibility, an admission route, an admissions committee and personal interview, and an English placement score. Its Bagrut-only exemption from psychometric study starts at average 92 generally and 95 for listed routes including secondary and special education. Its alternative Bagrut-plus-psychometric combined score is 540. Recognized preparatory courses have a separate route subject to college rules. Conditional Yael requirements apply to applicants whose Bagrut was not in Hebrew and who choose a Hebrew-language route; applicants with foreign Bagrut have separate requirements. The short public notes cite these central rules only within their stated scope; they do not assert that the nonformal specialization has a verified route-specific cutoff. The nonformal programme page does not publish its own admissions block, despite the central page saying route rules are on programme pages.

**Source discrepancy:** The special-education page's main admission block and the central admissions page both state 95 for the Bagrut route. A later exception paragraph on the programme page still says “below 92.” We use 95 for the standard route and do not derive an exception threshold from that paragraph. The combined 540 and committee route is preserved.
