A `below` result applies **only to the encoded route without psychometric scores**. It does not reject an applicant from BIU or assert that a composite route is impossible. Every scored explanation states that scope and points out other possible routes. Missing inputs request averages, subject records/grades or English classification, never סכם / מתואם / מצרף or `institution_weighted_score`.

## Encoded thresholds and formula

All rows also require English classification **85**, supplied through the existing `psychometricEnglish` input (psychometric English or Amirnet). No overall psychometric score is required by these routes.

| Programme | Bagrut average, inclusive | Subject gates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Law | 112 | English 5 units, grade 90 |
| Criminology | 98 | No additional subject gate in this table; second-major requirements remain to be checked |
| Life sciences | 108 | Either mathematics 5 units, grade 85; or mathematics units × grade ≥320 plus two science subjects at 5 units, grade 80 |
| Biotechnology | 108 | Mathematics units × grade ≥320 plus two science subjects at 5 units, grade 80 |
| Computational biology | 112 | Mathematics 5 units, grade 95 |

Science list exactly follows the table: biology, biotechnology, mathematics, chemistry, physics, engineering, computer science. Mathematics can count as one of the two science subjects when it meets 5×80; it still must satisfy the separate ≥320 product. Science subjects count once by identity, not by row count. Structured ordinary exams take precedence over legacy inputs; final projects/combined assessments do not masquerade as exams. Missing science evidence remains `needs_input` for a partial record and fails that route for a complete record that lacks two qualifying exams. Existing Hebrew wizard IDs for biotechnology and engineering are recognized without changing saved IDs.

No scaling, weighted combination, rounding or cutoff from the local legacy 560–685-scale thresholds is used. Formula confidence for composite: **unavailable/deferred**. Threshold provenance for the encoded Bagrut-only rules: **official**. Evaluation confidence: **estimated** because BIU-specific average replay is not proved. No exact comparison or 100% coverage claim.
